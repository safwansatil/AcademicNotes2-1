## FrameWork and Contracts

Frameworks are reusable designs that can be specialized to produce custom applications. Frameworks often include abstract classes that define the basic structure and behavior of the application, as well as concrete classes that provide specific implementations of that behavior.
Contracts define the obligations and guarantees of software components. They specify what a component expects from its environment (preconditions) and what it promises to deliver (postconditions). Contracts help ensure that components interact correctly and consistently, reducing the likelihood of errors and improving maintainability.
